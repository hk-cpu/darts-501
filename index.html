Camera settings

Higher sensitivity catches thin or dark darts but may pick up noise or shadows. Lower it if you get phantom darts.

Setup tips (read once)
  • Mount the phone rock-steady (tripod, clamp or shelf). If it moves even a little, recalibrate.
  • Position: about 0.5–1 m from the board, slightly below and/or to one side of the bull, tilted up at the board. Keep it out of the line of fire.
  • Whole board in frame, including the full double ring. Zoom (slider, +/− or pinch the preview) until the board nearly fills the picture, then calibrate. With digital zoom, drag the preview to centre the board. Changing zoom later means recalibrating.
  • Bright, even light: a ring light or lamps from the front. Avoid sun patches, flicker and lights that cast long dart shadows.
  • Calibrate by tapping the outer edge of the double ring at the top of 20, the right of 6, the bottom of 3 and the left of 11. Drag a dot to fine-tune; a magnifier helps you.
  • Throw, then step out of view. A dart is read once the picture has been still for about half a second.
  • Pull all darts after each turn. The app sees the board is empty and calls the next player. If it gets confused, press Board is empty · reset.
  • Keep the phone plugged in. The screen stays awake while the camera runs.
